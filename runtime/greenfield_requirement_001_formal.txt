Design a greenfield natural-gas injection surface process for an underground gas storage facility developed from a depleted gas reservoir in East China.

Pipeline-quality natural gas is supplied from a regional high-pressure transmission network.

Design basis:
- Feed pressure: 8.0–10.0 MPa
- Feed temperature: approximately 2 °C
- Design injection capacity: 700,000 standard m3/day
- Destination: underground gas storage injection wells
- The injection system shall be capable of delivering gas at an injection pressure of up to 35 MPa.
- Gas delivered to the injection wells shall not exceed 60 °C.

Determine a reasonable and feasible surface injection process based on these design conditions.

You must make the necessary process-design decisions yourself, including the required major process operations, their sequence, and reasonable intermediate process conditions.

Use the case name "Greenfield Injection Design Case" and Scope = Design for the design conditions represented in the model.
