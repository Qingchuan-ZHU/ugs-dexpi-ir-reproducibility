Design a greenfield natural-gas injection surface process for an underground gas storage facility with two injection-well groups operating at different pressure levels.

Pipeline-quality natural gas is supplied from a regional high-pressure transmission network.

Design basis:
- Feed pressure: 8.0–9.0 MPa
- Feed temperature: approximately 15 °C
- Total design injection capacity: 900,000 standard m3/day
- Injection Well Group A requires 300,000 standard m3/day at a design injection pressure of 20 MPa.
- Injection Well Group B requires 600,000 standard m3/day at a design injection pressure of 30 MPa.
- Gas delivered to either injection-well group shall not exceed 55 °C.
- The surface system shall be capable of supplying both well groups simultaneously at their stated design flowrates and pressures.
- This is a greenfield surface design; no existing compression or cooling equipment is prescribed.

Determine a reasonable and feasible surface injection process based on these design conditions.

You must make the necessary process-design decisions yourself, including the required major process operations, the branching arrangement, their sequence, and reasonable intermediate process conditions.

Use the case name "Greenfield Dual-Pressure Injection Case" and Scope = Design for the design conditions represented in the model.
